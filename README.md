# Chabad Chai Center: year end matching campaign progress

Public aggregate totals for the Power a Year of Jewish Life campaign
(December 15 to 16, 2026) shown on ChabadHome.com/DoubleImpact.

`progress.json` holds totals only: the amount given, the match (dollar for dollar,
up to the $109,000 matching pool), the combined total, gift count, donor count,
publicly recognized matching partners, the campaign window and the time of the
last update. It contains no donor names, emails or individual gifts.
